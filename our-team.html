About PHARMADICES

Learn about our mission, vision, and the dedicated team behind this educational project

Educational Project

PHARMADICES is a comprehensive medicine information website developed as an educational project by students of Government Post Graduate College (GPGC) Lakki Marwat, KPK, Pakistan.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, reliable, and comprehensive medicine information to healthcare professionals, students, and patients, promoting better understanding of pharmaceutical knowledge.

Our Vision

To become a trusted digital resource for medicine information, contributing to improved healthcare outcomes through education and awareness.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Database

Over 1000+ medicines with detailed information including uses, dosage, side effects, and precautions.

Advanced Search

Powerful search functionality to find medicines by name, brand, generic name, or medical condition.

Store Locator

Interactive map to locate medical stores and pharmacies across Pakistan, especially in Lakki Marwat.

Mobile Responsive

Fully responsive design that works perfectly on all devices including smartphones and tablets.
